Dwelling in the Fuchun Mountains

— a shrine, perpetually under construction, as all great works are —

In the last years of his life, an old man named Huang Gongwang walked the banks of the Fuchun River with a brush and a roll of paper and made the painting the Yuan dynasty is still remembered for. This shrine is a small, plain page for a very large obsession: one seven-metre scroll, the fire that nearly ate it, and the fool who painted it.
