Webring & Further Reading

This shrine is small on purpose. These aren’t.

Everything on this shrine is a plain retelling, kept short enough to fit a handscroll’s worth of patience. For the real depth — better reproductions, more careful scholarship, the connoisseurship arguments this shrine only gestures at — follow the ring onward:

  • Dwelling in the Fuchun Mountains on Wikipedia — the fullest general account of the painting and its fire, in one place.
  • Huang Gongwang on Wikipedia — more on the life sketched on the biography page here.
  • Four Masters of the Yuan dynasty — the wider circle Huang Gongwang painted alongside, all working outside the Mongol court’s patronage.
  • Handscroll — the format itself, and the arm’s-width viewing convention described on the scroll page.
  • National Palace Museum, Taipei — home today to the longer piece, The Master Wuyong Scroll.
  • Zhejiang Provincial Museum, Hangzhou — home today to the shorter piece, The Remaining Mountain, both described on the fire page.
